## 1. Engagement Models & Stages

Bangert Studio builds custom software with fixed-price or sprint-based contracts under Kazakhstan law (ТОО / ИП, ЭСФ, NDA).

| Project Stage | Multiplier | Typical Timeline | Description |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** | **0.7x** | 4 – 8 weeks | Rapid launch to validate hypothesis with core features on iOS, Android (Flutter) or Web. |
| **Mature / Enterprise** | **1.0x** | 8 – 24 weeks | Complete, production-grade architecture with advanced security, scalability, and deep integrations. |

- **Currency:** Contracts signed in **KZT (Kazakhstani Tenge)** for local clients or **USD** for international clients.
- **Payment Structure:** Sprint-based milestone payments (e.g. 30% advance, 70% upon sprint delivery).
- **Minimum Project Budget:** Starting from **$3,000 USD** (~1,500,000 KZT) for focused MVPs.
